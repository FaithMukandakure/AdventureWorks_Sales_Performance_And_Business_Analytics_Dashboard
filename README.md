# AdventureWorks | Sales Performance & Business Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing AdventureWorks' sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer purchasing patterns, geographic distribution, and product returns.

The dashboard transforms transactional data into actionable business insights to support financial performance monitoring, strategic decision-making, and operational improvements.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and analytical measures
- **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships between sales, products, customers, geography, and returns
- **Business Intelligence** – Performance reporting and trend analysis

## Dashboard 1: Executive Sales Overview

Provides a high-level view of business performance through financial and operational KPIs.

**Key metrics:**
- Total Sales: Approximately $25 million
- Total Orders: Approximately 56,000
- Quantity Sold: Approximately 84,000 units
- Total Profit: Approximately $10.27 million
- Return Rate: Approximately 2%

**Analysis includes:**
- Monthly and annual sales trends
- Sales versus profitability
- Sales distribution by product category
- Geographic sales performance
- Top 10 and bottom 10 products by sales

## Dashboard 2: Product & Customer Analysis

Examines product performance, sales concentration, and customer purchasing behavior.

**Key metrics:**
- Products Analyzed: 293
- Average Order Value: $444.54

**Analysis includes:**
- Product category and subcategory contribution to total sales
- Identification of top-performing and underperforming products
- Customer sales rankings
- Customer order counts and average order values
- Product-level sales and profitability comparisons

## Dashboard 3: Geography & Returns Analysis

Evaluates regional performance and product return patterns.

**Key metrics:**
- Countries Analyzed: 10
- Total Returns: Approximately 2,000
- Return Rate: Approximately 2%

**Analysis includes:**
- Sales and profitability by country
- Sales contribution by region
- Monthly product return trends
- Products with the highest return counts
- Returns by product category

## Key Business Insights

1. **Sales concentration:** Bikes account for approximately 95% of total sales, indicating significant dependence on one product category.
2. **Sales growth:** Monthly sales increased substantially during the later periods of the dataset.
3. **Geographic performance:** The United States and Australia are leading contributors to profitability.
4. **Product returns:** Accessories account for the largest number of returns, suggesting an opportunity for further product-quality and return-rate analysis.
5. **Customer performance:** Customer-level analysis identifies high-value customers and differences in purchasing behavior.

## Business Value

This project demonstrates how interactive business intelligence dashboards can help organizations:

- Monitor revenue and profitability
- Identify sales growth opportunities
- Evaluate product and customer performance
- Investigate return patterns
- Support data-driven financial and operational decisions

AdventureWorks sample dataset, used for educational and portfolio purposes.
